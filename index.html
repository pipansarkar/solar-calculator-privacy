Solar Calculator Privacy Policy

Effective date

24 November 2026. This policy covers the Solar Calculator Android app published by focustech.

What the app stores

Your calculator choices, application tracker, consumer and application numbers, vendor quotes, solar generation readings and warranty photos are stored on your phone. We have no server and do not receive this information.

Camera and photos

If you add a warranty document, your phone’s camera or photo picker gives the app only the image you choose. It stays in the app’s private storage.

Notifications

Reminders are created and delivered on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

Sharing

Information leaves the app only when you press Share and choose an app, such as WhatsApp or email. That app and the person or bank you send it to handle it under their own policies.

Backups

The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device transfer, so its data is not copied to a new phone. Use Share to keep your own copy of important documents.

Links

Official, bank and tariff links open in your browser or another app, under that site’s own policy.

No tracking

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or location access, and does not sell personal data.

Deleting your data

Delete individual items in the app, or clear the app’s storage or uninstall it to remove all data on the phone.

Children

The app is meant for adults planning a home solar purchase.

Not a government app

This is an independent app. It is not made by, or linked to, the Government of India, MNRE, the PM Surya Ghar portal, any DISCOM or any bank. Official information: pmsuryaghar.gov.in
